Insights

Performance Highlights

  • Fast reads: ListUser_Empty (29 μs) and GetUser (38 μs) are very fast.
  • Slow writes: CreateUser and UpdateUser are dominated by bcrypt hashing (~300 ms).
  • Rate limiter memory: BenchmarkRateLimit uses 289 KB/op — sliding window allocates heavily. Consider a token bucket for production.
  • Middleware chain bug: Full chain benchmark panics due to uninitialized logger in test context.
  • Repo writes: GORM Create/Update take ~400 μs vs reads at ~35-40 μs. Write path is 10x slower than reads.
