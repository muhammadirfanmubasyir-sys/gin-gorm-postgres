Key Findings

Performance

  • /health is very fast: 11,798 req/s (hey), 10,676 req/s (wrk). Sub-4ms average latency.
  • CRUD endpoints now perform well: wrk shows 634 req/s on /users with 100% success (no rate limiting).
  • Stable under constant load: vegeta at 50 req/s sustained for 20s with 100% success.
  • k6 p95 latency is excellent: 6.62ms overall, 4.77ms for successful requests only.

Rate Limiting Impact

  • With 1,000,000 req/min rate limit, CRUD endpoints no longer get 429s under moderate load.
  • Health endpoint bypasses rate limiting entirely (all tools show 100% success).
  • hey POST /users still shows 400x 429 due to high concurrency burst (25 concurrent creating many users rapidly).

Recommendations

  • The 1M rate limit is production-ready for moderate traffic loads.
  • Consider per-endpoint rate limiting if you need different limits for reads vs writes.
  • The health endpoint is production-ready for high-frequency health checks.
